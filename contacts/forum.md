The GAP project runs three mailing lists. For other ways to get help, see
[Help & Community]({{ site.baseurl }}/contact/).

### GAP Forum

<forum@gap-system.org> is for questions and discussions about GAP of
**general** interest. The GAP developers read it and answer; other
subscribers are invited to answer too. New releases of GAP are announced
here, as are bugs that lead to wrong results which cannot easily be
recognised as such.

**Subscribing.** Use the [web page](https://lists.uni-kl.de/gap/info/forum)
of the list, or send an email to <sympa@gap-system.org> with subject
"`subscribe forum`". To leave, use the subject "`unsubscribe forum`"; for
further commands, the subject "`help`". To change your email address,
unsubscribe the old address and subscribe the new one.

**Posting.** Only subscribers can post, and the mail must come from the
address ('From:' field) you subscribed with. This keeps junk mail out.

**Archive.** All posts since 1992 are public in the
[Forum Archive]({{ site.baseurl }}/forum/archive/).

### GAP development list

<gap@gap-system.org> is for discussions about the development of GAP and its
packages. It is open to everyone; subscribe on the
[web page](https://lists.uni-kl.de/gap/info/gap) of the list. Its
[archive](https://lists.uni-kl.de/gap/arc/gap) is visible to subscribers
only.

### GAP Support

<support@gap-system.org> is for questions that are unlikely to interest most
other GAP users, and for bug reports by email (see
[Reporting issues]({{ site.baseurl }}/issues/)). Anyone can write to it,
without subscribing. Messages are read by the Support Group listed below and
are not published. The list blocks attachments, so put log files and code
into the body of the email.

Please do not normally write to the individual members of the Support Group.
Each of them sees all mail sent to <support@gap-system.org>, a broader pool
of people sees it as well, and the answers are recorded so they can help
others in the future.
