Resources for learning GAP, working through examples, and using GAP in teaching.

- ### [Learning GAP]({{ site.baseurl }}/learn/learning/)
  In addition to the
  [Tutorial]({{ site.docsurl }}/doc/tut/chap0_mj.html),
  there is a variety of material intended to help people learn the GAP
  language and use the GAP system on their own. These resources are
  organized according to their level and intended audience.

- ### [Tutorials]({{ site.baseurl }}/learn/tutorials/)
  Explore mathematical examples step by step with GAP, starting with
  [Rubik's cube]({{ site.baseurl }}/learn/tutorials/rubik/).

- ### [Teaching Material]({{ site.baseurl }}/learn/teach/)
  GAP has been used in several places to support the teaching of a variety
  of courses. We are grateful to the colleagues who allowed us to point to
  their course material and would appreciate access to further material.
  These resources may help colleagues planning similar courses, as well as
  students studying the topics of such courses.
