This policy covers the websites `www.gap-system.org`, `docs.gap-system.org`
and `files.gap-system.org`.

We use no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. The pages of
`www.gap-system.org` load all their fonts, scripts and images from our own
server, so visiting them sends no data to third parties.

## Server logs

The server records requests in log files:

- The access log contains date and time, the page requested, the referring
  page and the browser configuration. It does not contain your IP address.
- The error log contains, for each request that fails, date and time, your
  IP address, the address requested and the referring page.

The logs serve to keep the server running, to find errors and to detect
attacks. They are deleted after seven days, are not combined with other
data and are not passed on. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR in
conjunction with § 3 of the data protection act of Rhineland-Palatinate
(LDSG).

## Data stored in your browser

- `www.gap-system.org` keeps your choice of colour theme in the local
  storage of your browser. It is not sent to the server.
- The manuals on `docs.gap-system.org` set the cookie `GAPDocStyle` if you
  select a display style other than the default. It holds the name of that
  style and expires when you close the browser.

## MathJax in the manuals

Each page of a manual on `docs.gap-system.org` exists in two versions. The
version with MathJax, reached via the link "MathJax on" and by some links
from this website, loads the MathJax script from `cdn.jsdelivr.net`. Your
browser then sends your IP address to
[jsDelivr](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net). The
version without MathJax loads nothing from third parties.

## Personal data published here

These websites publish

- names, and where provided email addresses and affiliations, of the
  authors of GAP, of the authors and maintainers of GAP packages, and of
  the members of the GAP Council and the Support Group;
- the [Forum Archive]({{ site.baseurl }}/forum/archive/), which contains all
  postings to the GAP Forum since 1992 with the names and email addresses
  of their senders.

Write to the contact above to have your data corrected or removed.

## Mailing lists

The [mailing lists]({{ site.baseurl }}/forum/) run on `lists.uni-kl.de`,
which is operated by RPTU. Subscribing stores your email address until you
unsubscribe. Postings are sent to all subscribers of the list, and those to
the GAP Forum are published in its archive. Mail to
<support@gap-system.org> is read by the Support Group and not published.

## Links to other services

These websites link to services we do not operate, among them GitHub,
which hosts the GAP source code, releases and issue tracker. Once you
follow such a link, the privacy policy of that service applies.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data (Art. 15),
to have it rectified (Art. 16) or erased (Art. 17), to restrict its
processing (Art. 18) and to object to it (Art. 21). Write to the contact
above.

You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77). The
one responsible for RPTU is the
[Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland-Pfalz](https://www.datenschutz.rlp.de).
